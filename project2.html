SipSpot Coffee Shops

View Code

  • JavaScript
  • MongoDB

SipSpot bridges the gap between large, established coffee brands and small, mom-and-pop stores across Florida. The app is built with Node.js and Express, with server-rendered EJS pages, a MongoDB database and a Mapbox map of the shops. Users can sign up to share comments and reviews about their favorite coffee shops and experiences. They can also purchase their preferred coffee beans and securely check out using Stripe.
